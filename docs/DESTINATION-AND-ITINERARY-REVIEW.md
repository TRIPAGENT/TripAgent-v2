# Destination fallback and itinerary review

## Open the customer itinerary preview

Open https://app.tripagent.vip/preview/itinerary after deployment, or choose “Explore a sample itinerary” from Journeys. Sign in with an active member code; a direct preview link returns here after sign-in. Locally, use the same route on the dev server.

The route is member-accessible and uses the same `Itinerary` component as `/journeys/:key`. An active member session is required. The five-night London sample is illustrative, with no invented prices, availability, flight times or checked entry rules. Plan, Days, Decisions, alternatives and saved selections are interactive. The pricing and Tara actions explain the next customer step without sending requests. This is a renderer preview, not an itinerary generated by the live model.

## Missing guides

- Unknown `/city/:slug` routes show a destination landing page.
- Submitting a search with no results opens `/destinations?q=...`.
- Catalogue matches use city names, country/region text, accents and conservative spelling tolerance. No arbitrary destinations are presented as nearby places.
- A known guide that fails to load offers a retry and other catalogue guides in its country.
- The lower Tara action carries the requested destination into an unsent draft, asking for dates, departure city, travellers and budget before planning.

## Research availability

The deployed backend's `/health` reported `researchReady: false` on 1 October 2026. It has a web-search tool, but `SEARCH_ENDPOINT` must be configured for live searches. No deployment credentials or backend settings were changed. The landing page therefore says web research is used when available.
